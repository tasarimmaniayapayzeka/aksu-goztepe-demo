    About the hospital

    We are a general hospital in Merdivenköy, a residential neighborhood of Kadıköy. Our 22 medical departments range from internal medicine and cardiology to pediatrics, general surgery and obstetrics. Imaging services, including CT, ultrasound, X-ray, mammography and Doppler, are available in the same building. Operating rooms, an intensive care unit, inpatient wards and a maternity service are here too.

    Our motto is "People first, health first". For us, this means listening without rushing, explaining each test and treatment in plain language, and making decisions together with you. Family members are part of this as well, and they have a right to clear information about their loved one's care.

    Emergency care, 24/7

    Our emergency department is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. You do not need an appointment, and you can come in at night, on weekends or on public holidays.

    If you think someone's life is in danger, call 112 first. It is the national emergency number in Türkiye, free of charge and available from any phone. Do not wait for an appointment if there is chest pain lasting more than a few minutes, sudden shortness of breath, weakness on one side of the body, slurred speech or a serious injury.

    How to book an appointment

    You can book an outpatient appointment by phone on +90 216 565 40 65 or by WhatsApp on +90 533 400 22 16. Tell us your name, the department or doctor you would like to see and the days that suit you. We will then confirm the date and time with you.

    Not sure which department you need? Describe your symptoms briefly and we will direct you to the right clinic. On the day, please arrive 15 minutes early and bring your passport or photo ID, a list of the medicines you take and any previous test results or medical reports.

    International patients and languages

    We welcome patients from abroad as well as foreign residents living in Istanbul. Please bring your passport to registration. If you have travel or international health insurance, ask us before your visit whether your policy can be used at our hospital.

    Each doctor's profile page lists the languages they speak, so you can choose a doctor you can talk to comfortably. If you would feel more at ease with someone to help you understand, you are welcome to bring a family member or friend. Medical terms can be confusing in any language, so feel free to ask your doctor to explain anything again.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need an appointment for the emergency department?

    No. The emergency department is open 24/7 and sees patients without an appointment. In a life-threatening situation, call 112 first so the ambulance team can start treatment on the way.

    What should I bring to my first visit?

    Bring your passport or photo ID, a list of your medicines with their doses, and any recent test results, scans or hospital reports. If you have health insurance, bring your policy details as well.

    Can I see a doctor who speaks my language?

    Start with our doctors page. Each profile lists the languages that doctor speaks. When you book, mention the doctor's name and we will look for a time that suits you.

    Where can I find my test results?

    Your doctor will go over the results with you at your follow-up visit. If you have a Turkish ID number or a foreign resident ID number, you can also view your laboratory results on e-Nabız, the Ministry of Health's online health record system.
